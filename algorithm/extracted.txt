Extra Pre-CAS questions that may or may not be asked
Personal / profile questions 
Tell me about yourself. 
What is your full academic background? 
When did you complete your last qualification? 
What have you been doing since then? 
Do you have any study gap? 
Why is there a gap in your education? 
Do you have any work experience? 
Is your work experience related to your chosen course? 
Have you ever applied to the UK before? 
Have you ever had a visa refusal? 
Have you travelled abroad before? 
Do you have any relatives or friends in the UK? 
Why are you applying now? 
Why this University?
What is the course start date and end?
Which modules will you study in the first semester and explain any one of them? 
Which module do you think will be the most difficult?  
How many teaching hours do you expect per week? 
How will you be assessed? 
Is assessment through exams, coursework, presentations, or projects? 
Why This Course?
How does the course at this university differ from similar courses elsewhere?How did you research this university, and what information influenced your decision most?
If another university offered the same course at a lower tuition fee, would you still choose this university? Why?
What specific facilities or learning resources at this university will support your studies?
How does the course at this university differ from similar courses elsewhere?
Who are going to sponsor you and how?
          What is your total tuition fee?
           How much have you already paid?
       What is your total expected living cost? 
Do you plan to work part time?
If you cannot find a part-time job in the UK, how will you support yourself?
Accommodation / UK living 
Where exactly will you live? 
What website did you use? 
How much is the rent? 
How far is it from campus? 
What public transport will you use?
UK knowledge 
What do you know about the city where you will study? 
What is the nearest major city? 
What is the nearest airport? 
What attractions or landmarks are nearby? 
What do you know about UK culture? 
How will you adapt to living independently? 
What challenges do you expect in the UK? 
How will you deal with homesickness? 
How will you manage your time between study and daily life? 
Tell me about your career objective?
What will you do immediately after graduation? 
Which companies in your home country would you apply to? 
What salary do you expect? 
Why would an employer pay you that amount? 
How will your UK degree help you compete in your local job market? 
Application-process questions 
Who helped you apply? 
Did you use an education consultancy? 
What role did the consultancy play? 
Did you choose the university yourself? 
Who prepared your SOP? 
Did you read your own SOP? 
Did anyone prepare your interview answers? 
Did you submit the application yourself? 
Do you know what documents were submitted? 
Have you read your offer letter? 
Have you read your CAS information? 
Credibility / pressure questions 
What if your visa is refused? 
What if you fail a module? 
What if you do not get a job after graduation? 
What if your sponsor cannot send more money? 
What if your university increases fees? 
What if you do not like the course after arriving? 
What if you get an opportunity to stay permanently in the UK? 
Would you still return home? 
Why should we believe you are a genuine student? 
What proves that your main purpose is education?
