# GGEC main topics, question angles and sub-questions

Existing database: 149 question rows (10 main, 139 reference/practice). This curated companion bank adds 40 alternative main wordings and 30 related practice prompts, without creating extra database rows. Each new interview selects one angle for each eligible main topic. Sub-questions are for optional self-practice, not automatically inserted.

## Why UK?

### Random interview angles

- What made the UK the right study destination for you?
- Which features of UK education matter most to your study goals?
- How does studying in the UK support your career plans?
- What benefits and drawbacks did you weigh before choosing the UK?

### Practice sub-questions

- What research influenced your decision?
- Which course feature supports the benefits you described?
- How would you explain the value of studying in the UK to your sponsor?

## Why not your home country?

### Random interview angles

- Why not study a similar course in your home country?
- What local study options did you consider before applying abroad?
- What would your chosen course offer beyond suitable options at home?
- If a similar course were available at home, why would you choose your UK option?

### Practice sub-questions

- Which local programme did you research?
- What specific learning opportunity influenced your comparison?
- Could you achieve your career goal through study at home? Explain your choice.

## Why not other countries?

### Random interview angles

- Which other countries did you consider, and why did the UK suit you best?
- How did you compare the total study costs across countries?
- How did course structure influence your choice between countries?
- What was the deciding factor when comparing the UK with other destinations?

### Practice sub-questions

- Which alternative destination was your strongest option?
- How did you check the information used in your comparison?
- What disadvantage of the UK did you consider?

## Why this university?

### Random interview angles

- What made your chosen university a good fit for you?
- Which features of your university directly support your study goals?
- What research convinced you to apply to this university?
- How does the university location suit your academic and practical needs?

### Practice sub-questions

- Which official page did you use for your research?
- Which facility or service would you use, and why?
- What did you learn about the department offering your course?

## Why not other universities?

### Random interview angles

- Which other universities did you consider, and why did you choose this one?
- How did you compare course content at different universities?
- How did fees and location affect your university shortlist?
- What was the deciding difference between this university and your next choice?

### Practice sub-questions

- What did your second-choice university offer?
- Which comparison mattered most to you?
- Where did you verify the course details you compared?

## Why this course?

### Random interview angles

- How does your chosen course build on your education or experience?
- Which skills do you want to develop through this course?
- Which course modules interest you most, and why?
- Why is this course the right next step towards your career?

### Practice sub-questions

- How would you use one of these skills in a future role?
- What have you researched about assessment on this course?
- What makes this course different from your previous studies?

## Tell me about your career objectives.

### Random interview angles

- What are your short-term and long-term career plans?
- How will your studies help you pursue your intended career?
- What role would you aim for after graduation, and why?
- What steps will you take to reach your career goal?

### Practice sub-questions

- Which skills would an employer expect for that role?
- What alternative plan would you consider if your first option did not work out?
- What research have you done about opportunities in that field?

## What have you planned for your accommodation?

### Random interview angles

- Where do you plan to live during your studies?
- How did you compare accommodation options near your university?
- How does your accommodation fit your budget and travel needs?
- What arrangements have you made for accommodation so far?

### Practice sub-questions

- What is the expected journey time to campus?
- What costs are included in the rent?
- What is your backup plan if your preferred accommodation is unavailable?

## Who is going to sponsor you and how?

### Random interview angles

- How will you fund your tuition and living costs?
- Who is supporting your studies, and how can they afford it?
- Can you explain your study budget and how you will cover it?
- What evidence supports your funding plan?

### Practice sub-questions

- How much tuition remains after any confirmed scholarship?
- How will you cover unexpected expenses?
- Which funds are currently available and how will you access them?

## Introduce yourself or describe yourself.

### Random interview angles

- Please introduce yourself and your academic background.
- Tell me about your education and what you are doing now.
- What experiences led you to your chosen field of study?
- How would you describe your study journey so far?

### Practice sub-questions

- Which experience most influenced your study plans?
- What have you been doing since your last qualification?
- What would you like to develop during your next course?
