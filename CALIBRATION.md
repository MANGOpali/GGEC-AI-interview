# Counsellor calibration exercise

The scoring-comparison screen supports independent counsellor scoring. It does not establish that the model is calibrated until counsellors contribute assessments. Do not fabricate human scores or adjust weights to fit a single interview.

## Procedure

1. Use fictional or consented completed attempts, and recruit a counsellor assigned to each student. Include multiple strong, weak, and contradictory answers across the categories; start with at least 20 paired answers as an exploratory exercise, not a statistical validation threshold.
2. Open **Scoring comparison** before reading the AI report. The endpoint returns no AI evaluation for unreviewed answers. Other authorised report screens still exist; independence depends on the reviewer avoiding them beforehand.
3. Review the saved profile, question and evidence. Give the four mandatory criteria a 0–10 rating; leave unrelated optional criteria unscored. Assess factual accuracy only where source evidence is sufficient. Classify the sample, submit, then view the AI comparison. Submitted ratings are immutable in this interface.
4. Review mean absolute difference (size of disagreement) and mean AI-minus-human difference (direction of bias), plus individual criterion differences. These are descriptive statistics, not an accuracy percentage.
5. Discuss large disagreements with a second counsellor. A second reviewer submits their own independent scores. Check whether the source material, prompt, or rubric is responsible. Document proposed changes and validate against a separate set of answers before changing weights.
6. Preserve the scoring version. Do not overwrite or silently rescore old interviews.

## Fictional exercise material (not pre-scored)

Profile: BBA graduate, MSc Business, aims to become a business analyst. No university-specific facts are verified by this document.
Question: How does this course relate to your previous education?

- Detailed sample: “My BBA introduced accounting, marketing and statistics. I want to build on the statistics work to analyse business performance. After checking the actual MSc module list, I would explain which module teaches the analytical methods I need and how I plan to use them as a business analyst.”
- Vague sample: “It is a good course, the university is famous, and it will give me a bright future.”
- Contradictory sample: “I have never studied business; my previous qualification was nursing, and my main goal is clinical practice.”

These are discussion prompts. A real counsellor must assign ratings; the labels are illustrative, not gold-standard scores.

## Verified course references

In Question bank, edit a question and attach a checked reference. Record university, course, intake, checked date, review-by date, modules, fees, relevant facts and an official HTTPS source. New sessions match university/course/intake exactly after trimming/case folding and snapshot that reference. Expired, unmatched or undated context does not support factual scoring. Fill the student's intake in My profile.

References are attached to individual questions, not automatically scraped or independently verified by the application. Keep each question's evidence relevant; do not paste unrelated course data into all categories. Human confirmation and source upkeep remain required.
